1ANTALYA, TÜRKIYE · CLIMATE CONFIRMED

Climate talks end in overtime with no deal

COP31 closed two days late with its cover decision not adopted: the presidency’s final package never mentions fossil fuels. The EU, UK and Panama objected; President Kurum told the plenary there was no consensus. (Times, Euronews)

2BENGHAZI, LIBYA · WAR REPORTED

Sea drones sink a Russian corvette in Libya

Suspected Ukrainian sea drones hit the Merkury at Benghazi naval base before dawn; two got through and satellite images show her capsized. About 10 were slightly hurt; Libya’s east asked the UN to investigate. (AP, Reuters)

3DYMER, UKRAINE · WAR CONFIRMED

Drone strike flattens flats near Kyiv; four dead

A Russian Shahed drone collapsed part of a five-storey block in Dymer. Four were killed and about 10 are still missing; a second strike on a Vyshneve grocery killed three. (Reuters)
